# Figure checklist

Storyboard: you can tell the paper's story with the figures alone, in order, to a colleague. Any figure outside that story is cut or moved to the appendix.

Each figure:
- [ ] Answers one question, which you can state in one sentence.
- [ ] Axes have labels, units, and symbols. Panels are labelled (A, B, C).
- [ ] All text is readable at final print size. Use a project template, not software defaults.
- [ ] Variation is visible: error bars or intervals shown, and noisy data not smoothed away. State what the bars mean (SD, SE, 95% CI) and n.
- [ ] Scales are honest: no truncated axis that inflates a small effect, unless that is the field convention and the caption says so.
- [ ] The caption works on its own. It says what is plotted, the conditions, n, what the bars mean, and the takeaway.
- [ ] It reads in grayscale. Use colorblind-safe palettes, with markers or line styles in addition to color.
- [ ] Every element earns its place. Remove chart junk, redundant legends, and default gridlines.
- [ ] Scale bars and annotations are added deliberately, not left as raw instrument output.
- [ ] The figure is referenced in the text, and the results paragraph narrates it.
